Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

RateSync: Currency Converter

Effective Date: October 24, 2026 · Developed by Lionz Studio

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Lionz Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application RateSync: Currency Converter ("the App", Package ID: com.ratesync.currencyconverter) on Android and other supported platforms.

Privacy-First Guarantee: RateSync is designed as a client-side utility. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information. Your currency watchlists, calculator inputs, rate alerts, and app preferences are stored 100% locally on your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure total user privacy, RateSync does NOT require you to create an account, register, or provide any of the following:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your real name, physical address, email address, or phone number.
  • No Payment Card Data: We never receive, process, or store credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account credentials. All transactions are securely handled directly through Google Play Billing.
  • No Precise Location Data: We do not access or track your GPS location.
  • No Contacts, Media, or Photos: The app does not request or access your contacts, camera, or personal media library.

3. Information Collected & Third-Party Service Providers

To provide real-time currency conversions, historical market charts, in-app billing, and crash diagnostics, RateSync interfaces with trusted third-party service providers as outlined below:

Service / Partner Purpose Data Handled
Google Play Billing Processing in-app purchases and subscriptions. Purchase transaction tokens, subscription state (managed entirely by Google).
RevenueCat In-app purchase validation and entitlement management. Anonymous App User ID, receipt validation tokens, active entitlement status (Pro).
Firebase Crashlytics (Google) Application crash reporting and stability diagnostics. Anonymized stack traces, device model, operating system version, and crash timestamps.
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) Displaying non-intrusive banner advertisements on select tabs to support free features. Advertising ID (AD_ID), device identifiers, coarse performance metrics, and ad interaction logs (managed under Google's Privacy Policy). RateSync Pro subscribers experience zero ads.
Public Currency Feeds (ECB / Open APIs) Retrieving live currency exchange rates and historical trends. Standard HTTP queries for currency pairings (e.g., USD, EUR). No personal identifiers attached.

4. Advertising & AdMob Policy Disclosures

To support the development and free availability of RateSync, we display banner advertisements powered by Google AdMob on the Charts, Alerts, and Profile tabs. The Converter tab remains strictly 100% ad-free.

  • Advertising ID Usage: Google AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / Android AD_ID) and non-personal diagnostic metrics to deliver, frequency-cap, and measure advertisements in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Ad-Free Experience with RateSync Pro: Users who upgrade to RateSync Pro enjoy an entirely ad-free experience. When Pro is active, all ad requests and SDK banner calls are suppressed.
  • Third-Party Privacy Policy: To learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Android Device Permissions & Usage

RateSync requests minimal platform permissions strictly necessary for core functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to query updated currency exchange rates and sync rates with market feeds.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively to deliver user-configured rate threshold alerts and notifications on Android 13+.
  • WAKE_LOCK & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Allows the background worker (WorkManager) to periodically check rate threshold alerts and refresh Android Home Screen widgets.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Required to facilitate optional in-app purchases for RateSync Pro.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Required by Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ to serve, frequency-cap, and detect fraud for ad impressions.

6. Local Storage & Security

Your customized preferences (such as selected base currency, favorite pairs, custom decimals, and home screen widget configurations) are saved locally on your device using encrypted storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences / KeyStore). This ensures your configuration remains secure and tamper-free on-device.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

RateSync is committed to full user data sovereignty and privacy transparency. Because RateSync does not require user accounts or store identifiable user profiles on external servers, managing or deleting your data is immediate, transparent, and user-controlled:

How to Delete Your Data:

  • Local On-Device Data: All currency watchlists, widget configurations, custom decimal preferences, and background rate alerts reside strictly on your phone. To permanently erase all data, open your Android device Settings → Apps → RateSync → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage, or simply uninstall the application. This purges 100% of local encrypted storage immediately.
  • Support Correspondence & Diagnostic Logs: If you have previously contacted customer support or submitted feedback and wish to request the deletion of your support emails or associated diagnostic logs, email us directly at lionzstudio.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process and permanently delete your email correspondence and records within 48 business hours.

* No user financial transactions or personal records are retained by Lionz Studio following a deletion request.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

RateSync is a general-audience financial utility and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate steps.
