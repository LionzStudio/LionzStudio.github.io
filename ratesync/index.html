RateSync: Currency Converter

By Lionz Studio Finance & Utilities ● Android App

RateSync is a sleek, modern currency converter designed for travelers, digital nomads, investors, and everyday users. Calculate foreign exchange across 150+ world currencies instantly, track multi-year historical trend charts, set background target rate alerts, and monitor your favorite pairs directly from your Android home screen.
